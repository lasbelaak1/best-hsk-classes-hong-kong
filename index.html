📚 What is HSK?

Why HSK Certification Is Your Career Catalyst

The Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi (HSK) is China’s official, globally recognized standard for Mandarin proficiency. With the new HSK 3.0 structure (9 levels across three stages), it rigorously tests real-world communication — from basic survival to executive-level nuanced discourse.

  • ✅ Unlock roles in finance, law, and tech across Central & Admiralty
  • ✅ Required for HKU, CUHK, and top mainland MBA programs
  • ✅ Objective, verifiable proof of fluency — not just "conversational"
